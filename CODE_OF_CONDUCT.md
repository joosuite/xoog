# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Xoog's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces should be able to contribute without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when disagreeing about technical choices.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect people's identities, boundaries, and privacy.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for harmful behavior.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexual harassment,
and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Report a concern

For content on GitHub, use the post's **Report content** menu. You may also use a
private contact method published on the
[repository owner's profile](https://github.com/joko76ers). Avoid sharing private
information in public reports. Security vulnerabilities should follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock discussions, and
restrict participation when necessary. Responses should reflect the severity
and recurrence of the behavior. These expectations apply to maintainers and
contributors alike.
